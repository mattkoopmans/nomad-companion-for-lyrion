PLUGIN_NOMADCOMPANION
	EN	Nomad Companion

PLUGIN_NOMADCOMPANION_DESC
	EN	Lets only the phone running Nomad Player control it. Other devices can still see what it's playing.

PLUGIN_NOMADCOMPANION_REFUSED
	EN	Only the phone running Nomad can control this player.

PLUGIN_NOMADCOMPANION_NOT_NOMAD
	EN	This only works on a Nomad player.

PLUGIN_NOMADCOMPANION_NO_PLAYER
	EN	That player isn't connected to Lyrion.

PLUGIN_NOMADCOMPANION_NOTHING_TO_PLAY
	EN	%s has nothing to play.

PLUGIN_NOMADCOMPANION_OTHER_NOMAD
	EN	Music can't be moved between two Nomad players.

PLUGIN_NOMADCOMPANION_BAD_MODE
	EN	The mode must be "replace" or "append".

PLUGIN_NOMADCOMPANION_TEST_MODE
	EN	Test mode

PLUGIN_NOMADCOMPANION_TEST_MODE_DESC
	EN	Lets the computer running Lyrion control Nomad players too, for testing the app from it. Leave this off otherwise.

PLUGIN_NOMADCOMPANION_SCROBBLING_TITLE
	EN	Scrobbling

PLUGIN_NOMADCOMPANION_SCROBBLING
	EN	Lyrion doesn't scrobble Nomad players, because it can't see what Nomad plays while offline. Scrobble from the phone instead: install the Last.fm app, sign in, and let it scrobble other apps, including Nomad. It asks for notification access to see what's playing, and keeps scrobbles made offline until the phone is back online.
